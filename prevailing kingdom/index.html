WELCOME TO OUR MINISTRY

A People of Purpose.
A Kingdom Without Limits.

Prevailing Kingdom Network Int'l is a Christ-centred, Spirit-led and Kingdom-focused interdenominational ministry established to raise a disciplined generation of prevailing armies who know Christ, walk in the power of the Holy Spirit, advance the Kingdom of God, and influence their spheres of life for God's glory.

We are committed to the Great Commission, spiritual transformation, Kingdom advancement, righteous leadership, and the demonstration of God's love and power.
